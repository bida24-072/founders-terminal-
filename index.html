CH. 02 — WHO I AM

A student.
A builder.
A Motswana.

I'm a Business Analysis & Computing student at BAC with a deep obsession for building things that live on the internet.

While most of my classmates focus on theory, I spend my evenings shipping real websites for real businesses — boutiques, restaurants, lodges, salons. Each project teaches me something new about design, code, and what Botswana actually needs online.

My goal is simple: prove that world-class digital work can come out of Gaborone.
